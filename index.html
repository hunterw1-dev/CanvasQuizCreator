Have AI write the questions

You'll copy a set of instructions from this page into an AI chat, then copy its answer back. It takes about two minutes. Prefer to write your own questions? Skip to step 4.

What's in your materials?
  1. Choose the AI tool you'll use.

    Not sure? Choose Copilot and sign in with your school Microsoft account. Keep student names and grades out of your materials.

  2. Copy the instructions.

    This copies your materials and point plan in one piece. You won't see anything change; it's saved to your clipboard.

  3. Open Copilot and paste.

    Open Copilot in a new tab
  4. Wait for the whole reply, then copy it.

    The reply will look like computer code, starting with {"questions". That's what you want. Wait until it stops writing, then click the Copy button on the code box.

  5. Come back to this tab and paste the reply here.

Something went wrong?
The AI says my message is too long, or won't send it.
Copy the short version below instead, which leaves out your materials. In the AI tool, attach your files with the paperclip or + button, paste the instructions, and send.
The reply stopped partway through.
Ask for fewer questions at a time. Lower the counts in step 2, copy and run it, and load those questions. Then set the counts for the rest, run it again, and choose Cancel when asked to replace, so the new questions are added to the end. Fix the counts back before you download.
The AI wrote a paragraph or asked me a question instead.
Reply to it with: Please give me only the JSON, with no other text. Then copy that reply.
Load questions shows an error.
Most often, part of the reply was missed. Clear the box, go back to the AI tab, copy the whole reply again, and paste it.

The key stays in this browser tab and is sent only to Anthropic. It is not saved; refreshing the page clears it.

Download for Canvas

      After importing, turn on Shuffle answers in the quiz settings so answer order differs for each student. The steps are below.

      How to import the file into Canvas
      1. Open your course and go to Settings, then Import Course Content.
      2. For Content Type, choose QTI .zip file.
      3. Choose the .zip you downloaded. Leave it zipped.
      4. If you use New Quizzes, check Import existing quizzes as New Quizzes when it's offered.
      5. Select All content, then Import. When the job says Completed, the quiz appears under Quizzes, unpublished.
      6. Open the quiz and turn on Shuffle answers. In Classic Quizzes, click Edit and check Shuffle Answers under Options. In New Quizzes, open the Settings tab and turn on Shuffle answers.
      7. Check the due date, time limit, and attempts, then Publish.

      If you converted an old quiz that has choices like "All of the above" or "Both A and B," edit those before you shuffle, or they'll stop making sense.
